Source: https://opengameart.org/content/3d-apocalyptic-building-city-cc0
Seven original Majadroid ruin variants converted to GLB.
The arena uses building 01 cut at source height 55 m and scaled to 32 x 20 m.
Its flat concrete roof keeps the original combat and climbing surface at Y=-0.5.
City meshes are decorative without physics; the original arena collision is preserved.
Backgrounds have no added lights, scripts, physics bodies or per-frame logic.

The lower ground plane has been removed. Existing lower 20 m sections are repeated downward nine times, preserving roof heights and window proportions. Arena physics is unchanged.

Skyline uses nearby low ruins and taller background towers on all sides for overhead and first-person views. Keep these varied heights when editing. Distance fog starts at 32 m and fully hides geometry at 180 m; height fog masks the extended lower floors.
