Source: 1K diffuse and OpenGL normal maps from Poly Haven.
Changes: resized to 128 x 128; diffuse quantized to 5 bits per channel.
Uses nearest filtering with mipmaps, a dark slate material tint, matte finish,
and a weak normal map to keep the PSX look and readable enemy silhouettes.
Diffuse contrast reduced to 55% around a neutral midpoint to keep enemy silhouettes readable.
